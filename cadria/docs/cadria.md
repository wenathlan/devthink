# cadria — the dossier

The video, image and 3D studio of the family. Cadria houses the VersaWaze engine at the folder root: the visual AI, the mesh generator, the shaders and the editing algebra.

## Identity

- Category: video / image player, editor and studio.
- Engine: VersaWaze (video, 3D mesh, shaders, visual AI).
- Competitor space: CapCut, After Effects.
- Consumer law: the 3D engine is imported by siblings (debonair imports cadria when it needs 3D), never duplicated.

## Objective

Turn every editing action into a real rendered artifact: the preview equals the final output, computed server-side in the saddle sandbox without touching the visitor GPU.

## Features

- Server-side rasterization with parallel sliced rendering: the preview is the final artifact geometry.
- The render job: real encoded bytes headless — PNG, GIF89a and WebM/EBML outputs with the verify twin (decode-back proof).
- Premiere-grade editing algebra: fifteen timeline operations with typed refusals.
- Mesh generation from images: luminance heightmaps become 3D meshes via the visualize library.
- Shader design seats and the visual AI generation lanes.
- Beatgrid and audio-visual bridges imported from debonair as a library (no duplicate audio logic).
- Color conversion, blend modes, ease curves and design motion seats.

## Interactions

- Timeline editing with instant preview (the sliced raster path).
- Export flows shipping real bytes (webm, gif, png) per render job.
- CLI console with the family chat design.

## Doctrine

- Never touch the visitor GPU: every render rides the saddle sandbox.
- The preview and the final artifact answer one geometry — the renderparity law.
