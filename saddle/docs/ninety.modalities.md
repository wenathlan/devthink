# ninety.modalities — the wave record

the ninety modalities wave (branch waved/ninety.modalities): the saddle
plane answers the ninety modality plan the owner charged — every planned
lane of the registry wires an honest executor, every app of the family
declares the modality its workloads ride, and the report never lies.

## the state the wave found

the registry carried the ninety records with 30 live, 3 host and 57
planned — the catalog promised more than the lanes wired. the edge plane
drove 16 lanes (10 live, 6 host) and every app of the family named its
sandbox seat only through the debonair precedent (4 ids).

## the wiring the wave answered

  (bubblewrap, nsjail, chroot with the fakechroot fallback and the

- **edgelandes.inproc.ts** (group b, 19 lanes): the ram recipes (tmpfs,
  mmap, overlayfs, loopram, gitram, zram with the real stored-block

- **edgelandes.ninety.ts** (the merge seat): the six heavyweight probes

- **modalityregistry.ts**: the honest flips — 51 planned lanes walk to

## the reconciliation with the parallel waves

the rebase onto main met two waves that landed while this one worked:
#239 (the tests home — the tests folders pure inside every app) and
#240 (the workload law — every app declares its lanes through
sandboxmodality.ts, the family seat with the join resolver and the
api routes). the reconciliation honored the no-duplication law: this
wave dropped its own app declaration seats (the modality.ts files of
group e) in favor of the #240 family seat the main branch pinned, and
kept everything the parallel waves did not touch — the fifty-seven
lane wirings, the registry flips, the vendor mirror sync and the
census pins. the two census rows #240 charged against the planned-only
gpu wait (the workload battery and the web workloads lane) now answer
the post-wave truth: the gpu records this wave wired live make the
stealthhead world-bake dispatchable, the family census carries zero
planned-only lanes, and the batteries say so.

  language-ruby precedent: live means the engine drives it when the

  executor: the webserver's plain node host resolves only .ts
  specifiers and the domain re-exports virtualgpu through a .js
  specifier — the family law (a domain the host cannot resolve degrades
  that one lane, never the engine boot) now pins the pattern for every
  future lane that reaches the .js graph.

  wiring of a confidential compute seat or a pod session is the next
  rung, and the registry says so.
