# infra.md — the official infrastructure document of the devthink family

the single source of consolidation for the whole infrastructure: the system, the applications, the features and the processes. it merges the official rascunho parts 01 to 07, the session 7 owner voice, the living rule manuals and the digests of the neodocs corpus into one file, organized per category and per application, with features and rules as the same thing. the owner statements are the source, session 7 prevails over the other sessions, and the tree wins over any document: skill first, tree, rules, voice, logs. nothing is invented; every rule rides the corpus. in conflict the more specific rule wins, ties break to the most recent changelog, and a divergence updates this file by bump.

## the objective

. devthink is the virtual operating system of the internet and the superplatform: it carries every application the person needs, it absorbs devthinkos, cli desktop, soflowx, akash, extension, gateway and maene, it embeds owni, and it competes with the whole market at once, the os, the browser, the chat and the cloud, with more than one million features of the frameworks, platforms, libraries, big techs, startups and open source projects it rivals, all self hosted.
. one application works fully at a time, devthink first, then the sub apps; the urgent items are the chat application fully functional opening and executing the sandbox, the vault storage built first because storage is of utmost importance, and the versawaze engine inside cadria as the real blender competitor.
. something is online before anything else is finished: a plain blank white page in english on the main domain, then the policy page and the terms page.
. authentication stays on the own self hosted infrastructure, never supabase as a service, because the own infrastructure has no limits; the repository still builds to cloudflare, netlify, vercel and github pages on the full domain, so no choice closes that path and nothing is hardcoded to one host.

## the operation

. two phases only, analysis first, execution second, never mixed; the fixed order is delete, read, authenticate, analyze, document, execute.
. reading is linear, hierarchical and alphabetical, top to bottom, never skipping; it rides the bash terminal with sed segments of line ranges, index first, details after, giant files in segments of one to four thousand lines with grep indexing the markers before the directed reading.
. delegation is open in waves of hierarchical subagents, two or three at a time for parallel work, up to the corpus scale of hundreds per wave; one category per agent, verification after every wave, subagents never commit, the orchestrator commits.
. every action of more than one hundred steps starts with a checklist of about one hundred items organized in waves; the master operation plans carry around one thousand sequential steps in twelve phases, and the version ladder carries the houses.
. work happens on the shared worklog, each agent writes its stage, a closed worklog is the done criterion, and the worklog never rides the repository.
. date first: consult the date and use the best standard and version of that date at each step, researching the newest patterns of 2026 along the way.
. verify per stage before advancing, verify real bytes before fixing apparent corruption, analyze failures from most recent to oldest; radical honesty reports the real state, the pendings and the plan, and a number only carries the measured label when it comes from real execution with origin and provenance recorded, otherwise it is declared simulated.
. fetch before and after every wave because the workspace can reset, verify the commit landed, then proceed; nothing is downloaded and no binary is installed, dependencies come from the global node modules or the cdn imports, no temporary files or folders exist, and clones land in the work root with the heavy ones deleted after the analysis.

## the structure law

. the repository root holds only app and mode folders plus docs, tests, forjas and loose files, and the monorepo carries the family apps with the envelope package.json at the root.
