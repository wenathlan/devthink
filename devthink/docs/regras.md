# regras

the official rules of the devthink operation. the owner statements are the source, session seven prevails over the other sessions, and this file maps every rule of the owner voice, of the owner rule file and of the official corpus, merged and deduplicated, nothing invented. the format obeys the writing rules themselves: dot hierarchy, lowercase, english, zero emoji, links and paths only in the last paragraph.

## objective

. devthink is the superplatform, the virtual operating system of the internet: it carries every application the person needs and competes with the whole market at once, the os, the browser, the chat and the cloud.
. the system is a mix of windows eleven, trille, brave, chrome and codex: the os wears the windows identity, the polish wears the trille ruler, the speed wears the brave and chrome grammar, the tooling wears the codex face.
. this is a real operating system, not a prototype and not a toy: every surface works end to end, every interaction answers, every feature is honest.
. the chat application is the urgent item: fully functional, it opens the sandbox and executes the sandbox, sustained by the saddle engine, with the design and the presentation icon redefined to the reference standard.
. one application works fully at a time: devthink first, then the sub apps, one at a time, never all at once.
. something is put online immediately, before anything else is finished: a plain blank white page in english on the main domain, then the policy page and the terms page.
. authentication stays on the own self hosted infrastructure, never a third party auth service, because the own infrastructure has no limits.
. later the repository builds to every platform, cloudflare, netlify, vercel and github pages, and serves the full domain, so no current choice may close that path: the infrastructure is universal, deploys anywhere, and nothing is hardcoded to one host.
. the configuration of external platforms is done through the owner real logged in brave browser, because the owner cannot configure those screens and is working on another task.

## operation

. two phases only, analysis first, execution second, never mixed, a phase closes before the next opens.
. the whole reading and the whole execution of an order ride one message, one wave sequence inside the current conversation: nothing is left for the next wave, the next message or the next session, and a promise of later is a defect, not a plan.
. the fixed step order: sync the remote first from the remote to the local, read the files, run the analysis, create the documents, execute.
. work in waves: each wave a selective group of changes with a commit at the end, waves contain sub waves, the delegation is open from zero to hundreds of sub agents, and there is no stopping.
. every action of more than one hundred steps starts with a checklist of about one hundred items, organized in waves, adapted to context, and the system itself follows the same rule.
. reading and execution are hierarchical, linear, top to bottom, one thing at a time, beginning, middle and end, connecting the points and the graphs of the whole project.
. date first: consult the date and use the best standard, the best pattern and the best version of that date at each step.
. delegate sub agents before executing inline, inline first, file last resort, clean the temporaries afterwards, no temporary files and no temporary folders at any moment.
. verify per stage before advancing, verify the real bytes before fixing the apparent corruption, analyze all failures from the most recent to the oldest, and verify in the browser, never only the status code: the render is the truth.
. radical honesty: report the real state, the pendings and the plan, never done without proof of render or a verifiable test, and a previous error is corrected for real.
. a number only carries the measured label when it comes from real execution with origin and provenance recorded, without that chain the result is declared simulated.
. the shared worklog is updated at each task, each agent writes its own stage, a closed worklog is the done criterion, and the worklog never rides the repository.
. nothing is downloaded and no binary is installed inside the work, the dependencies come from the global modules or the cdn imports, and the heavy builds run on the github computers, never on the local machine.
. fetch before and after every wave because the workspace can reset, verify the commit landed, then proceed to the next wave.
. sub agents never commit, the orchestrator commits, and design agents never touch workflows, changelogs or versions.
. nothing is deleted: duplicates keep a numeric suffix, retired infrastructure is kept marked as previous plan, never struck, never blank.
. clone and create everything inside the work root, free space before every clone, the corpus repository first and the platform repository after its analysis.

## reading

. the reading is integral, hierarchical and linear, top to bottom, never skipping a line, never jumping around.
. reading happens only through the bash terminal, in segments of line ranges, sed from a line to a line, index first, details after, giant files in large segments.
. the read tool is never used, by the agent or by any sub agent: the forbidden tools are read, chaos tools and results tools.
. the reading scope covers the uploaded files of the owner, the whole corpus repository of the owner with every tree and every historical commit cited, and the whole platform repository.
. the corpus is read file by file, folder by folder, commit by commit, hierarchically, in alphabetical order inside each folder, with nothing read twice for content: duplicates are extracted once and merged at the synthesis.
. every rule stated by the owner in the session files is mapped, nothing invented, and the oldest order gives way to the newest order of the same theme.

## writing and format

. everything lowercase, titles included, only code, file names and versions keep their own case.
. dot hierarchy only: point, subpoint, subpoint of the subpoint, no hyphen as marker, no numbered bullets, hyphen becomes dot in prose.
. one idea per sentence, clear, objective, progressive, connected and cohesive, impeccable spelling, grammar and punctuation, third person in all technical prose.
. one theme per sentence and per paragraph, the subject changes after the punctuation, never stacking subjects.
. duplicate words, duplicate senses and duplicate logics merge into one statement, one concept in one place, no synonym pair for the same thing, no contradiction.
. zero emoji anywhere, zero source citation, no document names, no line or origin numbering inside the text.
. all links, paths and commands live only in the last paragraph, the to visit list, and the body cites them by position.
. no verbosity in the interface text: short words, short sentences, one to five words per label, icons in place of text whenever possible.
. the two official documents of the operation are the rules file and the infrastructure file, both inside the docs folder of the os application, and the legacy mds of every app docs folder are removed once the two carry the whole truth.

## identity and naming

. the commit identity is the owner identity, never a machine identity, and every commit of every wave is signed.
. the signing key persists inside the repository so any session restores it, verifies the history and signs the next wave with the same lineage.
. the landing rides the verified vehicle: the wave lands on a branch, the pull request runs the battery, the squash merge signs the arrival, so the history of the main line answers verified.
. new names come from the real root of the technical vocabulary, slightly deformed, english base, sonority first, endings never repeated, validated in phrase, domain and brand.
. only official names, never an invented app, sub products never carry the platform name in the marketing name, and the reserved names are never reused.
. the brand keeps the capital letters in the presentation and the lowercase in the code and the paths, the system ai is doo, and the owner browser is named devthink.
. file names are lowercase, without underscore and without hyphen, short unique keywords, dot composition when a compound is needed, and the framework configs keep their own convention.

## structure

. the repository root carries only the app folders and the mode folders, plus docs, tests, forjas and the loose files.
. flat tree, no src, no folder inside folder by category, no globals, no lib, no hooks, no components folders scattered: folder equals page equals app.
. the theme folder is sol and there is no default theme folder: the theme anchor lives inside the theme beside the only css of the theme, and the app anchor is the only tsx outside the theme.
. the components folder of the theme carries every component of the application flat, no folder inside folder, in alphabetical order, integral and independent, with english names and the page prefixed dot composition, so the anchors consume them directly.
. the page folders keep only the anchor file, the anchor takes the name of the folder, imports the flat components of its page, and the theme anchor imports the page anchors.
. the pure logic rides the root of the app, always typescript, grouped by responsibility, and the interface pulls from the root, the root never pulls from the interface, the interface never pulls from the interface.
. every app carries its own docs and its own tests, the scripts live inside the tests, the family scripts live inside the os app, and no scripts folder rides the monorepo root.
. one manifest per language at the app root, the versions ride in lockstep, one lock only, and the dependencies are identical across the family, additive only, never a removal.
. no ui, assets, public, dist, data or db folders in the tree: the compilation and the artifacts stay outside the repository, only the icon and the favicon exist as assets.
. the same shape for every app, only the deploy copies of the theme differ, and the anchors adapt when a surface embeds inside a window.
. removed apps are forbidden to return: their features live absorbed in the roster.

## code

. typescript first, the runtime native before the external package, and the app is born a multi mode library.
. zero js in the interface, explicit ts in the relative imports, no enum, no namespace, strict mode, and the ignores are forbidden.
. nothing is hardcoded: the environment, the database, the host and the port are drawn and locked, never localhost, the code is the engine, the database is the living config, and the components read through typed accessors.
. the copy law: every interface text lives in the database of the own application, never hardcoded inside a component, tables first then ingestion, accessors typed like an api, and the seed rides the build.
. no fake data anywhere: no demo rows, no invented numbers, no theatrical timers, honest empty states only, and the ui never invents a datum.
. every component carries two logics, the animation one and the micro interaction one, because a component is a pre output of a feature.
. english in the code, in the jsdoc and in the copy, the translation rides the runtime api only when the person changes the language in the settings.
. biome is the standard formatter and linter, the conventional commits are enforced, the gates are the typecheck, the lint and the app test suite, and the tolerated errors are named.
. one file one responsibility, the correlated logics grouped in hierarchical blocks, and the big files stay organized by section banners.
. node orchestrates the engines of the manifest, the engines are libraries, and the per job rule picks the runner of the task.

## design

. the standard is windows eleven: the same look, the same interactions, the same animations, the same micro interactions, the same icons and the same quality, elevated to the professional saas refinement.
. every application matches the quality bar of the reference index, and an app below the bar is rebuilt, not patched.
. the lock screen and the authentication screen are identical to windows: the clock rides the left side of the lock face, the landscape fills the screen, the sign in answers inline, and the provider row continues with the platform name.
. one single design track for everything: only the presentation screen changes per platform, web, tv, binary, extension and mobile, the screens stay hidden until enabled, and the aspect ratio decides the device: landscape for tablet, tv and notebook, portrait for the phone.
. the icons are the fluent grammar, never the lucide react set, the size rides the font size prop, the primary action uses the filled variant, and the icon of each app carries the animated gradient inside the glyph with the layered stack.
. the settings carry the full personalization: the background of the desktop, the boot and the lock, the theme and the accent, the component toggles, the navbar position floating, left or right with the live re dock, and the options of the person.
. the ban list is permanent: no decorative dots, no white borders, no pill overdose, no transition of everything, no pure black and pure white fills, no emoji as icon, no text dissolving, no purple blue gradient defaults, no pastel landing, no three identical cards in a grid, no visible scrollbars where the chrome hides them.
. the motion families: swift micro feedback, patient entrances with stagger, long ambient loops, only transform, opacity and filter animate, and the reduced motion preference is always respected.
. generous touch targets, visible focus, the eight states of every interactive component, and the press answers with the scale gesture of the windows hand.
. the interface never shows the preface blocks that windows never shows: the chrome stays clean, the information density lives in the content, not in the frame.

## chat logic

. every user message is understood by seven independent sub agents and an eighth sub agent makes the final summary, then the output is produced.
. when the person edits a message the running work is never interrupted: a checkpoint is marked, a new session number opens, a parallel clone runs, the chain never stops, the checkpoints merge at the end, and the last edit prevails without losing any earlier detail.
. the conversation is born with the sandbox: the space, the sandbox, the session and the message identities are provisioned automatically, sustained by the saddle engine.
. the chat carries the terminal, the headless browser inspector and the code editor embedded, the approval modal precedes every sensitive action, and the kill switch is always available.
. the gateway is opt in: the natural state is disconnected, the assistant answers only after the person registers a gateway, never automatic.
. the chat grammar wears the reference composers, the avatarless timeline, the reasoning expander owning its hue, the day separators, the draft chips and the honest token meter.

## data and storage

. the database is the virtual repo: it mirrors the repository content per theme, the code, the assets and the information, and every application owns its database.
. the hd is the database: the person opens as many hd databases as wanted in the settings, like the drive panel of windows, and the external storages connect opt in.
. one login across the family: the account of any app is recognized in every other app at once, the databases talk over https, the same identity, the same callbacks.
. the blobs never ride the database raw: the chunks carry the checksum, the compression and the catalog with the automatic reuse.
. the storage engine of the platform is the vault: every app imports the storage from it instead of reimplementing, and the site roles of the network materialize the storage, the execution and the joined pair.
. the database is never readable or exportable by the stranger, the registered people only, and the static deploy never exposes a downloadable database: the decoy serves the anonymous probe while the real one stays sealed.
. the replication rides the write quorum, the read of any healthy copy, and the idempotent daily reconciliation.

## sandbox and zero touch

. the interface never writes in the browser storage of the visitor, never uses the visitor gpu, cpu, camera or geolocation, and never compiles on the machine of the person: only the interface loads locally.
. everything rides the domains, the mimetypes and the https protocol, the modalities run on the runner sites, and the platform resolves the join of all as the nuclear modality.
. the saddle engine concentrates the modalities of the sandbox toward dozens: the complete virtual machine, the universal runner of any binary, the node packages, the hardened container, the immutable image, the micro vm per workflow, the browser and the virtual browser, the mobile, the confidential with attestation, the qemu and the firecracker, the wasm and the wasi, and the vfs born from the database and the git objects.
. the engine lifecycle is a serializable job with the event trail, the retryability code on failure, and the cleanup in the end, and every execution emits the honest receipt: the storage latency is never labeled as memory.
. the isolation ladder denies by default: the untrusted rides the isolate, the compiled rides the wasm, the boundary rides the micro vm, and the node vm is never a boundary.
. zero captcha anywhere and the credentials of every generated app are ephemeral, scoped and own, never the account of the person.

## accounts payment and plans

. the signup is email and password or google, github and gitlab, the single callback page detects the origin, and without login the temporary identity persists, never deleted.
. the cli is the web: the same design, the same account, the same signup, and the sync is constant in both directions.
. the tiers free, pro and enterprise live only inside the os application, the other applications never carry the paywall, and the store sells the pre trained ais, the plugins, the themes and the runners.
. the monetization rides the dedicated site, the invisible click is the forbidden traffic, and the ethic is absolute.
. the platform serves humans and virtual users, the ai is a registered user with the server always on, and the hierarchy of the creators governs.

## build release and git

. the build, the test and the hash run only on the github computers, the workflows are declarative yml, one per responsibility, grouped by stage, and the heaviest app rides last.
. the release fires on the version bump of the manifest or the changelog change, is immutable, and carries hundreds of assets, for example three hundred to four hundred: the binaries, the extensions, the containers of every external application plus the assets of every sub app.
. the iso is exclusive to the os application because only the os is an os.
. the official compression is the tar with the max level plus the brotli over it, and the checksums are generated by the pipeline, never hardcoded.
. every app ships to the registries: npm, the github npm, the container registry, nuget, maven and rubygems, idempotent, with the existence check.
. the workflows stay green: the bug is fixed at the root, the version bumps until the pipeline answers green, and no wave opens on a red pipeline.
. the pins ride the version tags, never the digest, the main line is protected, the branches archive after the merge, and the ladder of the houses climbs to the certified stamp.
. the same tree serves every target, the web, the pages, the mobile, the desktop and the iso, the descriptors ride the theme folder, and the static only deploy never carries functions.

## security

. the secrets never ride the code, the environment is ignored, the rotation is standing, and the tokens in the database ride the authenticated encryption.
. every query is parameter bound, every request validates the host, the localhost and the private ranges are refused, and the family domains only answer the cors.
. the passwords ride the memory hard function, the sessions ride the rotating tokens, the oauth rides the state and the proof key, and the ai never participates in the authentication.
. the devtools intruder is blocked aggressively: the ip and the dns register forever, and every connected site blanks for that person.
. the consent gates are three steps, deny by default, the kill switch is always available, and the sensitive classes ask the fresh consent per origin.
. the corpus secrets stay outside every read, merge and rewrite, inventory only, never emitted.

## applications roster

. devthink is the os and the superplatform: it engulfs the retired apps, embeds the gallery, owns the browser name, carries the native pages of the theme, and creates no media logic of its own because it imports the published engines of the family.
. saddle is the sandbox and vm engine: virtualization only, the complete runner of any market sandbox, the virtual hardware of the domains, no browser, no gateway, no os features.
. debonair is the audio and the daw: the creation of audio and music, the katexis engine at the root, importing the gateway and the media engine when it needs them.
. cadria is the video, the image and the three dee: the player, the editor and the studio, the versawaze engine at the root, absorbing the retired media apps.
. stealthhead is the first person shooter: it ships pre compiled so the visitor hardware never compiles, importing the engines of the family.
. argan is the dns and network library: zones, records, dnssec, the registrar panel and the sovereignty model.
. getry is the deployable gateway: the inference of the network with the meta model masking the real models, raising copies of itself to sustain the concurrency, always the free tag models.
. vault is the storage engine and the database clone of the platform: all the storage lives in it and only in it.
. forge and foundry are the site roles of the network: the first runs only, the second runs and stores, both born from the saddle engine, existing as sites with the libraries published.
. cobaia is the monetization surface: the ads, the plans and the conversion, kept apart so the main sites stay clean.
. the extension is the consent first browser agent of the family, the cli is the terminal face of the same design, and doo is the universal system ai.
. one house per logic family: the image and video come from cadria, the audio from debonair, the sandbox from saddle, the storage from vault, the gateway from getry, and the logic is never duplicated.
