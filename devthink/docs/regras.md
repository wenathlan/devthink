# regras

the official rules of the devthink operation. the owner statements are the source, session seven prevails over the other sessions, and this file maps every rule of the owner voice, of the owner rule file and of the official corpus, merged and deduplicated, nothing invented. the format obeys the writing rules themselves: dot hierarchy, lowercase, english, zero emoji, links and paths only in the last paragraph.

## objective

. devthink is the superplatform, the virtual operating system of the internet: it carries every application the person needs and competes with the whole market at once, the os, the browser, the chat and the cloud.
. the system is a mix of windows eleven, trille, brave, chrome and codex: the os wears the windows identity, the polish wears the trille ruler, the speed wears the brave and chrome grammar, the tooling wears the codex face.
. this is a real operating system, not a prototype and not a toy: every surface works end to end, every interaction answers, every feature is honest.
. the chat application is the urgent item: fully functional, it opens the sandbox and executes the sandbox, sustained by the saddle engine, with the design and the presentation icon redefined to the reference standard.
. one application works fully at a time: devthink first, then the sub apps, one at a time, never all at once.
. something is put online immediately, before anything else is finished: a plain blank white page in english on the main domain, then the policy page and the terms page.
. authentication stays on the own self hosted infrastructure, never a third party auth service, because the own infrastructure has no limits.
. later the repository builds to every platform, cloudflare, netlify, vercel and github pages, and serves the full domain, so no current choice may close that path: the infrastructure is universal, deploys anywhere, and nothing is hardcoded to one host.
. the configuration of external platforms is done through the owner real logged in brave browser, because the owner cannot configure those screens and is working on another task.
. the own infrastructure costs nothing because it is ours: no paid service, no paid virtual server and no domain purchase ride the platform, and only the binaries, the packages, the repository platforms with their pipelines and the sandbox serve as dependencies.
