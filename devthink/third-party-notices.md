# DevThink third-party notices

DevThink includes or builds with third-party components. Their individual
licenses remain in force. Package manifests, lockfiles and generated
license inventories are the source of truth for the exact dependency set.

## Merged lineage notices (the 2.0.0 grand merge)

The 2.0.0 grand merge joined four repositories into one product under
GPL-3.0-only. Three of the absorbed lineages were originally published
under the MIT license; their original grants are acknowledged here so the
lineage license history stays recorded:

- **gateway** (io.github.wenathlan:gateway, @wenathlan/gateway, v1.1.13) —
  the embedded provider gateway engine, MIT, "Copyright (c) 2026 wenathlan".
  Its release history is preserved verbatim in the CHANGELOG.md lineage
  sections.
- **extension** (@wenathlan/extension, v2.0.13 — the DevThink browser
  extension and agent surface, GPL-3.0-only at merge time) — the dominant
  code mass of the merged product; its GPL grant carries unchanged.
- **maene** (@wenathlan/maene, v2.1.16 — the Antigravity engine) — MIT,
  "Copyright (c) 2026 maene contributors". Its release history is preserved
  in the CHANGELOG.md lineage sections.
- **devthink** (@wenathlan/devthink, v1.1.16) — the workbench core, MIT at
  merge time; the merged product supersedes its grant with GPL-3.0-only.

Every file, feature and document of the four repositories carries into the
merged product readapted to the DevThink metadata.
