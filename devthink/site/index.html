The audio DAW of the OS — generation, editing and mastering on the katexis engine. Like suno × FL Studio.

Player, editor and studio for video, image and 3D on the versawase engine. Like after effects × figma × blender.

stealthhead

FPS game platform — match, ranking, weapons and world logic on versawase.

The system network library: zones, records, DNSSEC and handshake. The DNS engine every site shares.
