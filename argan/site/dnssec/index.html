dnssec · pipeline-only

DNSSEC

Every published zone is signed on day one — real signatures, real validation, no opt-in. Signing is a pipeline stage: build, test and hash happen in CI, and the signed zone is what gets served.

the two keys

KSK signs keys, ZSK signs zones

KSK

360-day rollover

The Key Signing Key signs only the DNSKEY RRset. Its DS hash is what the parent zone carries — the anchor of trust for everything below it.

devthink.pro. IN DS 48213 15 2 9f3a…

ZSK

90-day rollover

The Zone Signing Key signs every RRset — A, MX, TXT, and the wildcard that carries the hung model. Short-use key, rotated quarterly, cheap to replace.

Long guard versus short use follows NIST SP 800-81r3.

algorithms

What new zones are signed with

ED25519 · alg 15 ECDSA P-256 · alg 13 RSA/SHA-256 · alg 8 NSEC3 · SHA-256 digest

ED25519 is the default for every new zone: small signatures, fast verification, 256 bits. ECDSA P-256 covers validators that predate algorithm 15; RSA/SHA-256 survives only where a legacy parent demands it. Denial of existence uses NSEC3 with a fresh random salt on each republication, and the DS handed to the parent uses the SHA-256 digest (algorithm 2).

rule ND-6002 · pipeline-only

Never hand-edit a production zone

The zone file is versioned, signed and published by the pipeline with a fresh serial — the previous version stays available for instant rollback. Nobody types changes into a live zone.

$ argan sign --zone devthink.pro --ksk ksk-2026q1 --zsk zsk-2026q1
zone      devthink.pro          serial 2026011202 -> 2026011203
dnskey    KSK 257 3 15 (ED25519) + ZSK 256 3 15 (ED25519)
rrsets    412 signed, NSEC3 salt rotated
transfer  NOTIFY -> ns2.devthink.pro, AXFR complete
status    published

rollover

Key rotation, four moves

ZSK every 90 days, KSK every 360 days. CDS/CDNSKEY automation hands the new DS to the parent without a registrar ticket.

1 · Pre-publish

T−30d

The successor key is generated and published next to the incumbent. Both validate; nothing changes for resolvers yet.

2 · Switch

T0

Signatures move to the successor. Signatures made by the incumbent keep validating until their TTL expires.

3 · Retire

T+TTL

The incumbent stops signing. On KSK rollover the parent DS is swapped automatically via CDS/CDNSKEY.

4 · Remove

T+2 TTL

The incumbent leaves the DNSKEY RRset and the database; the audit log records the retirement.
