argan.devthink.pro

Names that resolve.

argan is the DNS and gateway library of the DevThink OS: authoritative zones and record sets, a signing pipeline with real DNSSEC, handshake over GNS and PKARR, and the hung model — personal domains anchored on the devthink.pro apex, published to the world through ordinary DNS. No plugin, nothing installed on the visitor's side.

the library

One library, four jobs

Every site in the family consumes argan by configuration — no fixed address, port or credential in code, and the zone database is shared across the OS.

Zones & records

authoritative

Master and slave zones, record types behind factories, wildcards, serials in YYYYMMDDNN and transfer by NOTIFY + AXFR to the secondaries.

DNSSEC pipeline

signed day one

KSK and ZSK with ED25519, NSEC3 denial of existence, rollover by CDS/CDNSKEY — and a hard rule: production zones are never hand-edited.

Handshake & transports

GNS · PKARR

GNS petnames (RFC 9498), PKARR signed packets published to the DHT, and every DNS transport from classic 53 to DoT, DoH, DoQ and ODoH with automatic fallback.

The hung model

vhost at the apex

A bought domain anchors everything: personal subdomains hang from it with a wildcard to the same edge, vhost by Host header, live URL in seconds.

Anchor a name on the apex

npm install --global @devthink/argan && argan publish --label mysite --zone devthink.pro
